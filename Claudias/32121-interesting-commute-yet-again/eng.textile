Interesting commute yet again

Author: Claudias

*000*2016-07-19 11:38

Hey guys it's been a while since I've posted. This is just a wierd little experience I encountered on the commute home this weekend after a few drinks. It takes me a subway train and bus to get to and from the downtown core of Toronto each anywhere from 25 to 45 minutes.

Now the other night I'd been out for a pint with my friend in the city and I just managed to catch the last subway. Nothing really happened on the subway it was pretty empty several for an old couple and a group of 20 somethings (3 girls one guy) obviously heading home after a few drinks. I was mostly sober but two of these girls were not. It's not that they were slurring or stumbling they were just being kinda loud laughing and singing. Anyways the subway pulls into the station and I hope off and head up to my bus which happens to pull in just as myself and this group now behind me are coming up the escalator.

We get on the bus but it's on a 10 or 15 minute holding period before we leave. After a few minutes I can hear the girls talking behind me(I'm not eavesdropping) literally half the bus could probably hear what they were talking about but one of them is debating making a run for the bathroom before we leave as she's had to go since they left the bar. Just as she's starting to lean towards going the driver jumps in and we're off. Along the journey this girl breaks conversations with her friends occasionally to remind them and apperently me and half the other people on the bus that she indeed still has to pee. Suddenly the bus screeches to a halt. The driver had to break to avoid some idiot on his cell making a turn the girl gasped and started whispering to her friends.

I couldn't make out what she was saying, not that I wanted to I don't like to listen in on people. But I had my suspicions as she went from being the life of the party to quiet as a mouse. It was a few stops later when the passed my seat to get off that my suspicions were confirmed as she walked by I couldn't help but notice a dark patch all over the back of her jeans. I guess the stop startled her, but she seemed a good sport about it they were kinda laughing as they got off the bus.

Anyways I'm sorry it's not much of a story but I hope you enjoyed
