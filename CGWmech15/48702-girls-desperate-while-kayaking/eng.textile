Preface: my highschool graduating class was 7 kids from a very small town in Arizona. I'd been in class with the same kids since 4th grade and I absolutely had to leave. Through a friend of a friend of a friend I landed a job as a heavy equipment mechanic in Alaska 3 months before I finished highschool. I took it in a heartbeat and moved out 2 days after I graduated.

I came from a family with a decent amount of money and my dad had bought me a used toy hauler to live in until I found a more permanent living situation. I'd been in Alaska 2 months, just moved into an apartment when my bosses daughter and some friends asked if we could take my trailer to Fairbanks to camp for the weekend. It slept 8 comfortably so I agreed and we set off. Myself, another mechanic friend of mine, my bosses daughter, and 3 of her friends that were all girls. My buddy and I shared the bed in the front and the girls took the bunks and couches in the back. Tight living quarters for a co-ed group that wasn't romantically involved, but we had fun for the weekend.

One night we decided to go kayaking, and it ended up going very well for an omorashi fan such as myself. I was 18, my mechanic friend was 22, and the girls were 17, 19, 19, and 20 at the time. He bought us a 30 rack of Coors light, we rented 3 two man kayaks and hit the Chena River. Before we set out we parked my truck a few miles down the river and the 17yo girl agreed to be our DD, mostly because other than myself she was the only one that could drive stick. I ended up paired with my bosses daughter in one kayak, the 2 younger girls were together, and my buddy was with the oldest girl that he'd been after for awhile.

Beer goes down easy when it's 85 out and you're floating down a river and soon I had to pee. I pushed our kayak to the side and took a leak next to my buddy while the girls complained that they couldn't go. Few more beers down and we stopped at a park along the river so the girls could go. Naturally the bathrooms were closed, and they complained more.

More cruising down the river, my buddy and I pulled over to pee again and all 4 of the girls were getting really desperate. My bosses daughter had her hand buried in her crotch and wouldn't touch the paddle anymore. Feeling compassionate I tied the kayak with both girls to mine and towed them so they wouldn't have to paddle anymore. Almost an hour and a half later and we reached my truck, all 4 girls desperate to go. My truck only actually seated 5, so the 17yo took the drivers seat, my bosses daughter sat on my lap in the front seat, and the remaining 3 piled in the back seat. The whole drive she had her hand in her crotch, grinding on my leg, absolutely desperate to get to the bathroom.

10 minute drive and we were back at my trailer, but there was only one bathroom. The girls played a quick match of rock, paper, scissors to see who could go first. My bosses daughter lost and was dead last even though I could tell she was there most desperate. I sat on my couch, while the DD(the only girl that didn't drink alcohol) went first. My bosses daughter came and sat on my lap again, grinding into my leg once again. 2 more girls went before her and I really didn't think she was going to make it. RIGHT before the last girl came out of the bathroom she squeeled, and doubled over in my lap and I honestly thought she'd popped, but she ran into the bathroom and came out appearing dry. Before each one went I got to watch all of them do their own pee dance on floor of my trailer. Absolutely adorable.

Forever greatful for getting to see 4 girls bursting while my buddy and I relieved ourselves freely on the riverbank.

Got to see my bosses daughter desperate once more on a long drive, and I ended up getting loosely involved with one of the other girls about a year later. She held for me once right before she started flight attendant school and I'll share that story later.
